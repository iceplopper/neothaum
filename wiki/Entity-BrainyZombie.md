The **Angry Zombie** is an entity added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Spawning

**Angry Zombie** spawns in dark conditions or near ambient aura nodes.

## Drops

When slain by a player, a **Angry Zombie** can drop:

- Elemental Crystal Shards
- Experience Orbs
- Rare Knowledge Fragments or Curios

## Behavior

The **Angry Zombie** exhibits specialized AI routines faithful to Thaumcraft 4.2.3.5:

- **Scanning**: Players can target this entity with a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to unlock research aspects associated with its spiritual nature.
- **Combat / Interactions**: Attacks players or provides autonomous base maintenance depending on its alignment.
