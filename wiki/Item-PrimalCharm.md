A **Primal Charm** is an item added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Obtaining

### Crafting

| Recipe File | Type | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `primalcharm.json` | Arcane_Shaped | Order Shard, Balanced Shard, Gold Ingot, Air Shard, Water Shard, Earth Shard, Entropy Shard, Fire Shard |

## Usage

The **Primal Charm** serves several thaumaturgical purposes:

- **Thaumometer Scanning**: Players can hold and scan this item using a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to discover and earn research aspect points.
- **Alchemical Slurry**: Smelting this item in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) breaks it down into pure liquid essentia according to its constituent aspects (None known / Compound derived upon scan).

### Crafting Ingredient

**Primal Charm** is used as a crafting material in the following recipes:

- [Focal Manipulator](Item-FocalManipulator) (`neothaum:focal_manipulator`)
- [Wand Focus: Primal](Item-WandFocus:Primal) (`neothaum:focus_primal`)
- [Wand](Item-Wand) (`neothaum:wand`)
