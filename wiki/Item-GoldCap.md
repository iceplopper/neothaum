## Overview

The **Gold Cap** is an item introduced by **NeoThaum** belonging to the Wands & Casting Implements family.

| Property | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **English Name** | Gold Cap |

| **Chapter Alignment** | [Thaumaturgy](Thaumaturgy) |
| **Stack Size** | Up to 64 (or 1 for tools/armor/foci) |

---

## How to Obtain

Crafted in an [Arcane Worktable](Arcane-Worktable) or vanilla Crafting Table using metal nuggets, gems, and primal shards.

---

## Usage & Mechanics

The Gold Cap is utilized throughout Thaumaturgical research and operations:
- Can be scanned using the [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to unlock its constituent spiritual aspects.
- Can be dissolved in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) to extract raw liquid Essentia.
- Functions as a vital component in higher-tier thaumic engineering recipes.
