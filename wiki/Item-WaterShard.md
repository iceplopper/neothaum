A **Water Shard** is an item added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Obtaining

### Mining

**Water Shard** drops when mining naturally generated elemental Infused Stone ore deposits with any pickaxe, or from slaying specific elemental entities.

## Usage

The **Water Shard** serves several thaumaturgical purposes:

- **Thaumometer Scanning**: Players can hold and scan this item using a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to discover and earn research aspect points.
- **Alchemical Slurry**: Smelting this item in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) breaks it down into pure liquid essentia according to its constituent aspects (**Praecantatio** (1), **Aqua** (2), **Vitreus** (1)).
