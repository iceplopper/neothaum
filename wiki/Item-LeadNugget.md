A **Lead Nugget** is an item added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Obtaining

### Crafting

| Recipe File | Type | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lead_nugget.json` | Crafting_Shapeless | #C:Ingots/Lead |
| `translead.json` | Crucible | Special recipe |

## Usage

The **Lead Nugget** serves several thaumaturgical purposes:

- **Thaumometer Scanning**: Players can hold and scan this item using a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to discover and earn research aspect points.
- **Alchemical Slurry**: Smelting this item in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) breaks it down into pure liquid essentia according to its constituent aspects (**Metallum** (1)).
