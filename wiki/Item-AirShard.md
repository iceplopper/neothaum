The **Air Shard** is an item introduced by **NeoThaum** belonging to the Materials & Alchemical Reagents family.

## How to Obtain

Mined from subterranean Infused Stone deposits with a stone or iron pickaxe, or obtained from mob drops.

---

## Usage & Mechanics

The Air Shard is utilized throughout Thaumaturgical research and operations:
- Can be scanned using the [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to unlock its constituent spiritual aspects.
- Can be dissolved in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) to extract raw liquid Essentia.
- Functions as a vital component in higher-tier thaumic engineering recipes.
