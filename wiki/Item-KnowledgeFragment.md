The **Knowledge Fragment** is an item introduced by **NeoThaum** belonging to the Research & Knowledge family.

## How to Obtain

Crafted via [Arcane Worktable](Arcane-Worktable), [Crucible Alchemy](Crucible-Alchemy), or [Infusion Altar](Infusion-Altar) rituals.

---

## Usage & Mechanics

The Knowledge Fragment is utilized throughout Thaumaturgical research and operations:
- Can be scanned using the [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to unlock its constituent spiritual aspects.
- Can be dissolved in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) to extract raw liquid Essentia.
- Functions as a vital component in higher-tier thaumic engineering recipes.
