A **Beef Nugget** is an item added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Obtaining

### Natural Occurrence & Discovery

**Beef Nugget** can be obtained through thaumaturgical synthesis, world exploration in dungeon barrows, research unlocks, or mob drops.

## Usage

The **Beef Nugget** serves several thaumaturgical purposes:

- **Thaumometer Scanning**: Players can hold and scan this item using a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to discover and earn research aspect points.
- **Alchemical Slurry**: Smelting this item in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) breaks it down into pure liquid essentia according to its constituent aspects (**Fames** (1)).

### Crafting Ingredient

**Beef Nugget** is used as a crafting material in the following recipes:

- [Triple Meat Treat](Item-TripleMeatTreat) (`neothaum:triple_meat_treat`)
- [Triple Meat Treat](Item-TripleMeatTreat) (`neothaum:triple_meat_treat`)
- [Triple Meat Treat](Item-TripleMeatTreat) (`neothaum:triple_meat_treat`)
