A **Magic Tallow** is an item added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Obtaining

### Crafting

| Recipe File | Type | Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `magic_tallow.json` | Crafting_Shaped | Tallow Block |
| `tallow.json` | Crucible | Special recipe |

## Usage

The **Magic Tallow** serves several thaumaturgical purposes:

- **Thaumometer Scanning**: Players can hold and scan this item using a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to discover and earn research aspect points.
- **Alchemical Slurry**: Smelting this item in an [Alchemical Furnace](Essentia-Distillation) breaks it down into pure liquid essentia according to its constituent aspects (None known / Compound derived upon scan).

### Crafting Ingredient

**Magic Tallow** is used as a crafting material in the following recipes:

- [Tallow Block](Item-TallowBlock) (`neothaum:tallow_block`)
- [White Tallow Candle](Item-WhiteTallowCandle) (`neothaum:white_tallow_candle`)
