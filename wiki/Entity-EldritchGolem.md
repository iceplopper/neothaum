The **Eldritch Golem** is an entity added by **NeoThaum** (recreating Thaumcraft 4 for modern Minecraft).

## Spawning

**Eldritch Golem** does not spawn naturally. It is constructed and deployed by players using a [Golem Placer Poppet](Item-GolemPlacer) or bound via Crucible alchemy.

## Drops

When slain by a player, a **Eldritch Golem** can drop:

- Elemental Crystal Shards
- Experience Orbs
- Rare Knowledge Fragments or Curios

## Behavior

The **Eldritch Golem** exhibits specialized AI routines faithful to Thaumcraft 4.2.3.5:

- **Scanning**: Players can target this entity with a [Thaumometer](Scanning-&-Thaumometer) to unlock research aspects associated with its spiritual nature.
- **Combat / Interactions**: Attacks players or provides autonomous base maintenance depending on its alignment.
