# NeoThaum 1.0.4-alpha — Initial release

A modern Fabric recreation of Thaumcraft 4 (4.2.3.5) for Minecraft 26.3, by Ice Plopper. Built with AI coding assistants (Claude and
Gemini), checking each system against the original TC4 source and testing it in automated in-game scenes. **Alpha:** expect bugs, and back
up your worlds.

Requires Fabric Loader 0.20.1+, Fabric API and Java 25. Optional integrations: JEI, Jade, ModMenu, Xaero's World Map.

## Major features

- **Aspects, scanning and research.** All TC4 aspects and their combinations, item/entity/node aspect tables, the Thaumometer and Goggles
  of Revealing, the Thaumonomicon with TC4's research tree and pages, the research table and research-note minigame, and knowledge
  fragments.
- **Wands, staves and foci.** Wand caps and rods, 3D wand rendering, staves and sceptres, the arcane worktable, every wand focus with the
  radial focus menu, focus pouch and focal manipulator.
- **Alchemy.** The crucible, alembics and alchemical furnace (and the advanced furnace), warded jars, essentia tubes, buffers and
  reservoirs, the centrifuge and crystallizer, the Thaumatorium, flux goo and gas.
- **Infusion.** The runic matrix, pedestals and pillars, instability events and infusion enchanting.
- **Aura nodes and the vis network.** Every node type and modifier, node in a jar, stabilizers, transducers, vis relays, energized nodes,
  node-driven taint, and land changes around sinister, pure and tainted nodes.
- **World generation.** Greatwood and silverwood trees, the Magical Forest, Tainted Land and Eerie biomes, ores and crystal clusters,
  mana pods, eldritch rings with altars and obelisks, barrow mounds, hilltop stones, and wizard towers and banker homes in villages.
- **Mobs.** Wisps, firebats, pechs, angry and furious zombies, thaumic slimes, the whole taint family, the Crimson Cult (knights, clerics
  and the Praetor), eldritch guardians, crabs, the Eldritch Warden and Golem, and the cultist portal boss.
- **Champion mobs.** TC4's whitelist and odds, the 13 modifiers with their real effects, champion bosses, and loot bag drops.
- **Golemancy.** Golems in every material, cores, upgrades and decorations, the golem GUI, traveling trunks, and the Golemancer's Bell with
  markers.
- **Warp.** Permanent, sticky and temporary warp, warp events (hallucinations, mind spiders, eldritch gazes, research unlocks), warp ward,
  bath salts and the arcane spa.
- **Gear and artifice.** Thaumium, void metal and fortress armour and tools, elemental tools, robes, Boots of the Traveller, the
  Thaumostatic Harness, baubles with runic shielding, lamps, the arcane door, warded glass, keys, the Infernal Furnace, the hungry chest,
  the arcane bore, mirrors, and the deconstruction table.

## Minor features

- **Fluids.** Liquid Death (finite fluid, dissolves creatures into crystallized essence) and Purifying Fluid (flowing fluid that grants
  warp ward), with buckets.
- **Items.** Primal arrows and the Bow of Bone, the Crimson Blade and cultist gear, alumentum, loot bags, the Sinister Lodestone, the
  sanity checker and bottled taint.
- **Drops.** Aspect orbs from slain creatures and banners.
- **Ethereal Bloom.** Purifies taint around it.
- **Configuration.** ModMenu screen with 0–10 multiplier sliders (1 = TC4) for nodes, worldgen, taint, flux, the infernal furnace, aspect
  orbs and wisps, plus toggles.
- **JEI.** Arcane, crucible, infusion, infusion enchanting and aspect pages.
- **Jade.** Node info when the node is scanned and seen through goggles or a thaumometer.
- **Xaero's World Map.** Scanned aura nodes on the map, with a toggle in Xaero's button column.
- **Add-ons outside TC4.** Copper golems, a shovel orientation preview, vanilla candle support and advancements.
- **Polish.** Five creative tabs, translations for 22 languages, TC4 sounds, particles and block/entity renderers.

## Known issues

- The Eldritch Portal leads nowhere yet (the Outer Lands are not ported).
- The golems' Use, Liquid and Essentia core AI is incomplete.
- Wizard and banker villagers, apprentice's rings and the harness model are not ported.
