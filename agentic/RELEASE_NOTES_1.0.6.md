# NeoThaum 1.0.6 (alpha)

A large pass towards a 1:1 Thaumcraft 4 recreation. Includes the Outer Lands from 1.0.5.

## Major features

- **Golemancy rebuilt from TC4's code.** Every core now runs TC4's own task list: fill, empty, gather, harvest (with replanting from an order upgrade), guard, liquid, essentia, lumber, use, butcher, sorting and fishing. Golems use TC4's carry limits, range, speed, regeneration, upgrade effects and eight GUI toggles. Their home container is the block a golem was placed against. Golem darts and the fishing bobber are new, and the golem screen follows TC4's layout and rules.
- **Bosses rebuilt from TC4's code.** All bosses now share TC4's boss behaviour:
  - they turn on whoever has hurt them most
  - they grow stronger with more players in the fight
  - they enrage when struck for more than 35
  - they regenerate slowly
  - **Eldritch Warden:** a ward that regrows, a sapping field trail, ring "frenzies" when the ward breaks, eldritch orbs and its screech.
  - **Eldritch Golem:** tramples soft blocks and loot, launches what it hits, and loses its head instead of dying, then fires orbs.
  - **Crimson Praetor:** TC4's gear, a regeneration aura for cultists, and orbs only at range.
- **Village wizard and banker.** The wizard's tower and the banker's house now have their villagers, with TC4's trade lists and skins.
- **Arcane Bore works.** It now finds its base. Its dig timing, spiral, fortune and silk-touch drops, native-cluster chance and tunnel lighting all follow TC4.

## Minor features

- **Thaumonomicon:** the Crafting Sceptres entry is added. The goggles helm, the three fortress masks and the advanced golem have their infusion recipes. The non-TC4 copper golem page is removed.
- **Apprentice's Rings:** all six primal rings, found in chests and sold by the wizard.
- **Warp visuals:** the heartbeat and Death Gaze vignette. Death Gaze, Blurred Vision, Unnatural Hunger and Sun Scorned have their screen shaders.
- **Thaumostatic Harness:** its backpack model, plus the lightning ring and sparks while hovering.
- **Eldritch Guardian:** TC4's orb and screech attacks, half damage from projectiles, and its gaze fog.
- **Cultists:** they call every nearby cultist for help. The cleric keeps its spells for targets two or more blocks away.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - The last Giant Taintacle of a group drops a Primordial Pearl.
  - Golem orbs use TC4's homing and damage.
  - Banners use up the whole essentia phial.
  - The copper golem no longer presses buttons on its own.
